**Class heading (中文)**

皮革和人造皮革，不属别类的皮革和人造皮革制品；毛皮；箱子和旅行袋；雨伞和阳伞；手杖；鞭和马具。

**Class heading (English)**

Leather and imitations of leather, and goods made of these materials and not included in other classes; animal skins, hides; trunks and travelling bags; umbrellas and parasols; walking sticks; whips, harness and saddlery.

> A China application covers **10 items per class** (extra items are charged individually), and protection generally runs per sub-class rather than across the whole class — see [the class selection guide](../guides/05-nice-classification-guide-china.md).
