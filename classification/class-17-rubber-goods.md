| **橡胶制品** | **Rubber goods** |

**Class heading (中文)**

橡胶、古塔胶、树胶、石棉、云母，以及不属别类的这些原材料的制品；生产用成型塑料制品；包装、填充和绝缘用材料；非金属软管。

**Class heading (English)**

Rubber, gutta-percha, gum, asbestos, mica and goods made from these materials and not included in other classes; plastics in extruded form for use in manufacture; packing, stopping and insulating materials; flexible pipes, not of metal.

> A China application covers **10 items per class** (extra items are charged individually), and protection generally runs per sub-class rather than across the whole class — see [the class selection guide](../guides/05-nice-classification-guide-china.md).
