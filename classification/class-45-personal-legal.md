# Class 45 — 社会法律 / Personal and legal services

[← Class 44](class-44-medical-beauty-agricultural.md) · [All 45 classes](README.md)

> **Nice Classification, Class 45** — 6 sub-classes (类似群) and **291 goods/services items**, Chinese–English, as used by CNIPA (China). Part of the [45-class bilingual classification dataset](README.md) from [china-trademark.net](https://china-trademark.net/).

| 中文名称 | English heading |
|---|---|
| **社会法律** | **Personal and legal services** |

**Class heading (中文)**

法律服务；为保护财产和人身安全的服务；由他人提供的为满足个人需要的私人和社会服务。

**Class heading (English)**

Legal services; security services for the protection of property and individuals; personal and social services rendered by others to meet the needs of individuals.

> A China application covers **10 items per class** (extra items are charged individually), and protection generally runs per sub-class rather than across the whole class — see [the class selection guide](../guides/05-nice-classification-guide-china.md).
