**Class heading (中文)**

咖啡，茶，可可和咖啡代用品；米；食用淀粉和西米；面粉和谷类制品；面包、糕点和甜食；食用冰；糖，蜂蜜，糖浆；鲜酵母，发酵粉；食盐；芥末；醋，沙司（调味品）；辛香料；冰。

**Class heading (English)**

Coffee, tea, cocoa and artificial coffee; rice; tapioca and sago; flour and preparations made from cereals; bread, pastry and confectionery; edible ices; sugar, honey, treacle; yeast, baking-powder; salt; mustard; vinegar, sauces (condiments); spices; ice.

> A China application covers **10 items per class** (extra items are charged individually), and protection generally runs per sub-class rather than across the whole class — see [the class selection guide](../guides/05-nice-classification-guide-china.md).
