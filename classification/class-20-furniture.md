# Class 20 — 家具 / Furniture

> **Nice Classification, Class 20** — 14 sub-classes (类似群) and **754 goods/services items**, Chinese–English, as used by CNIPA (China). Part of the [45-class bilingual classification dataset](README.md) from [china-trademark.net](https://china-trademark.net/).

| 中文名称 | English heading |
|---|---|
| **家具** | **Furniture** |

**Class heading (中文)**

家具，镜子，相框；不属别类的木、软木、苇、藤、柳条、角、骨、象牙、鲸骨、贝壳、琥珀、珍珠母、海泡石制品，这些材料的代用品或塑料制品。

**Class heading (English)**

Furniture, mirrors, picture frames; goods (not included in other classes) of wood, cork, reed, cane, wicker, horn, bone, ivory, whalebone, shell, amber, mother-of-pearl, meerschaum and substitutes for all these materials, or of plastics.

> A China application covers **10 items per class** (extra items are charged individually), and protection generally runs per sub-class rather than across the whole class — see [the class selection guide](../guides/05-nice-classification-guide-china.md).
