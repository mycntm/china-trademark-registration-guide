## 📋 Core Services (China)

All filings are made **directly with CNIPA** by a China-licensed agent. English communication throughout; the official fee is always included in the quoted price.

- **Trademark Registration** — USD 199 per class (10 goods/services included, +USD 10 per extra item). No Chinese company or address needed.
- **Trademark Renewal** — every 10 years, renewable indefinitely.
- **Trademark Assignment / Transfer** — buy or sell registered Chinese marks.
- **Licence Recordal** — register your brand-licensing deal with CNIPA.
- **Recordal of Changes** — name or address changes on existing registrations.
- **Opposition & Invalidation** — fight conflicting applications and registrations.
- **Non-Use Cancellation (3-year)** — clear squatters blocking your brand.
- **Refusal Review / Appeal** — respond to provisional refusals, including Madrid refusals.
