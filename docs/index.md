## Nice Classification for China (China trademark classification)

The classification China actually examines against: **45 classes, 495 sub-classes (类似群) and 32,665 goods and services items**, each with its official Chinese wording and a matching English name.

Every class has its own page, grouped by sub-class rather than listed flat — because within a single class, items sitting in different sub-classes are generally *not* treated as similar goods, and that is what decides whether a 10-item filing actually blocks anyone else. The same data ships as CSV and JSON under CC BY 4.0: mirror it, ship it inside your own tools, cite it.

[Browse the 45 classes →](classification/index.md)

## Quick answers

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Cost per class | **USD 199** — 10 goods/services items and the CNIPA official fee included |
| Extra items in a class | USD 10 each |
| Time to registration | **7–9 months** in practice; **12–15 months** if every statutory stage runs to its limit |
| Protection term | **10 years**, renewable indefinitely |
| Do I need a Chinese company? | **No** — a foreign company or individual can own a Chinese trademark |
| Opposition window | 3 months, **2 months from 1 January 2027** |
| Paris Convention priority | Claimable within 6 months of your home filing |

## The one thing most applicants get wrong

China divides each of the 45 classes into **sub-classes (类似群)**. Two items in the same class but different sub-classes are generally *not* considered similar goods — so a 10-item selection that all sits inside one sub-class protects almost nothing else in that class.

That is why every class page here is grouped by sub-class rather than listed flat, and why the [class selection guide](guides/05-nice-classification-guide-china.md) exists.
